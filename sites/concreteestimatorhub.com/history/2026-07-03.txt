# Concrete Calculator Hub

> Free concrete estimating tools for volume, bag count, slabs, footings, steps, ready-mix comparisons, and material cost planning.

Concrete Calculator Hub helps DIY homeowners, small contractors, publishers,
and material planners estimate concrete quantities before ordering bags or
ready-mix concrete. The site focuses on practical material estimates, not
structural engineering advice.

Use the calculators for quick planning, then confirm product yield, delivery
minimums, site conditions, local codes, and contractor or engineer guidance
before buying concrete or starting regulated work.

## Core Calculators

- [Concrete Volume Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-volume-calculator): Estimate cubic yards, cubic feet, cubic meters, bags, waste, and cost from project dimensions.
- [Concrete Bag Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-bag-calculator): Convert project dimensions into whole 40 lb, 60 lb, 80 lb, or metric concrete bag counts.
- [Concrete Slab Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-slab-calculator): Calculate patio, driveway pad, garage slab, shed base, and other flatwork concrete volume.
- [Concrete Footing Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-footing-calculator): Estimate continuous footing volume from run length, width, depth, waste, and cost assumptions.
- [Concrete Steps Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-steps-calculator): Estimate concrete for stairs and steps from tread, rise, width, step count, and waste.
- [Concrete Cost Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/concrete-cost-calculator): Compare bagged concrete and ready-mix material totals.
- [Ready-Mix vs Bags Calculator](https://concreteestimatorhub.com/ready-mix-vs-bags-calculator): Compare delivered ready-mix concrete with bagged concrete for small projects.

## Specialized Calculators
